Privacy Policy

Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you visit or use our website:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address
  • Payment details (processed securely via trusted third-party providers — we do not store card data)
  • Website usage data collected via cookies and analytics tools
  • Marketplace reports you upload to our Meesho and Myntra reconciliation software (orders, payments, returns and inventory)
  • Contact details and chat messages processed through WOO Chat on behalf of your business

How we use your information

  • Process and deliver your orders accurately and on time
  • Provide customer support and resolve queries
  • Improve our website, services, and overall user experience
  • Send updates, offers, or marketing emails — only if you have opted in

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party. We may share your data only with:

  • Trusted payment gateways to process transactions securely
  • Shipping and delivery partners to fulfil your orders
  • Government or legal authorities as required by law or to protect our rights

Seller reports and WhatsApp data

Reports you upload to the Meesho and Myntra reconciliation software are used only to calculate your profit and show it back to you. We do not sell them or share them with any marketplace or other seller.

For WOO Chat, your customers' numbers and messages are processed only to send the messages you set up, through the official WhatsApp Business API from Meta. You stay in control of your customer data.

We keep this data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by writing to support@esowolf.in.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and track website performance. Cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it.

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Some features of our website may not function properly without cookies.

Your choices

  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Data security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our payment processing is handled by trusted, PCI-compliant third-party gateways.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and keep your account information confidential.

Grievances

If you have a concern about how your personal data is handled, write to us at support@esowolf.in with the subject "Privacy grievance". We will acknowledge it within 24 hours and work to resolve it as quickly as possible.

Refund Policy

Service-based refund policy

All services offered on esowolf.in are non-tangible, customised, and initiated based on client requirements. Due to the nature of digital and professional services, refunds are handled under the following conditions:

  • Once a service has been started, initiated, or delivered — it is non-refundable
  • Payments made for consultation, onboarding, setup, digital services, or marketing services are non-refundable once work has commenced
  • Refunds will not be issued for delays caused by incomplete information, lack of response, or changes requested by the client

Eligibility for refunds

A refund may be considered only if all of the following conditions are met:

  • The service has not yet been started
  • The refund request is made within 24 hours of payment
  • No resources, tools, or workforce have been allocated to the project

Approval of refunds is at the sole discretion of esowolf.in after internal review. Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval.

Refund process

  • You will be notified about the approval or rejection of your refund request
  • If approved, the refund will be processed to your original method of payment within 7 business days of approval

The one exception: if we fail to deliver what we explicitly promised, you are covered by our Return Policy, even after work has started.

Non-refundable services

  • Digital marketing services
  • E-commerce setup or onboarding services
  • Website development or customisation work
  • Advertising management services
  • Consultation or strategy sessions
  • Subscription or recurring service fees once activated

Cancellation policy

Clients may request cancellation of a service before work begins. If approved, any applicable refund (if eligible) will be processed after deducting administrative or transaction charges incurred on our end.

To request cancellation, contact us at support@esowolf.in as early as possible. Once work has begun, cancellations are not eligible for refunds.

Refund processing timeline

  • The refund will be processed to the original payment method
  • We process approved refunds within 7 business days of approval; your bank or payment provider may take a few extra days to show the credit
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the refund has been initiated
