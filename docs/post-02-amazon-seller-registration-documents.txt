FAQ:
Q: Can I do Amazon seller registration without GST?
A: Only if you sell products that are fully exempt from GST, such as most printed books. For almost every other category, Amazon asks for a valid GSTIN during registration.

Q: Can I use my savings bank account for Amazon?
A: Amazon pays your sales money into the bank account you add. Many small sellers use a current account in the business name, but check what Seller Central accepts for your business type when you register.

Q: Does the PAN have to match the GST details?
A: Yes. Your GSTIN is linked to a PAN, and the name and details you enter should match your GST certificate. Mismatches are a common reason for delays.

Q: How long does Amazon take to verify my documents?
A: The registration form takes about an hour if your documents are ready. Amazon may take a few working days to verify GST and other details, so plan for that.

Q: Do I need a trademark to sell on Amazon India?
A: No, you can start selling without one. A registered or applied trademark is needed only if you want to join Amazon Brand Registry for your own brand.

## What Amazon seller registration needs from you

When you sign up at sell.amazon.in, Amazon wants to confirm three things: who you are, that your business is allowed to sell, and where to send your money. Every document on the list below helps Amazon check one of these points.

The good news is that you do not need a big company or an office. Many sellers start from home with a proprietorship. What matters is that your details are correct and match each other everywhere.

## The Amazon seller documents checklist

Here is everything you should keep ready before you start. Use it as a tick list.

| Document or detail | Why Amazon needs it | Tips to avoid delays |
|---|---|---|
| GSTIN (GST number) | To confirm your business can sell taxable goods | Needed for most categories. Check that your GST status is active |
| PAN card | Your tax identity, linked to your GST | Use the PAN that your GSTIN is registered under |
| Bank account details | Amazon pays your sales money here | Account name should match your business or PAN name |
| Mobile number | Login and one-time passwords (OTP) | Use a number you will keep for the long term |
| Email address | Seller Central login and Amazon messages | Use a business email you check every day |
| Business name and address | Shown on invoices and used for pickups | Write it exactly as it is on your GST certificate |
| Digital or scanned signature | Needed to accept the seller agreement and for invoices | Sign on white paper and keep a clear photo or scan ready |
| Product details | You must list at least one product to go live | Keep product photos, MRP, HSN code and GST rate ready |

If you plan to sell only GST-exempt products, such as most books, Amazon lets you register without a GSTIN. For everything else, get your GST number first. Without it, you will be stuck at the tax details step.

## GST for Amazon sellers: what you should know

GST is the step where most people pause. Amazon asks for your GSTIN because online sellers who supply taxable goods through a marketplace need GST registration under Indian tax rules. Amazon also collects a small Tax Collected at Source (TCS) on your sales and reports it to the GST system.

A few practical points:

- **Get GST before you register.** Applying for GST on the government portal can take some days. Start early.
- **Check your principal place of business.** The address on your GST certificate becomes your business address on Amazon.
- **Add the right business type.** Proprietorship, partnership and private limited companies can all sell. Choose the option that matches your GST certificate.

If you are not sure which GST rate or HSN code applies to your products, ask your accountant. A wrong rate on your listings can cause problems later.

## Step-by-step: how Amazon seller registration works

Once your documents are ready, the registration itself follows a simple path:

1. **Create your account.** Go to sell.amazon.in, click "Start Selling" and sign up with your email and mobile number.
2. **Enter your business details.** Add your legal name, business type and address.
3. **Add your tax details.** Enter your GSTIN and PAN, and wait for Amazon to read your GST details.
4. **Add bank details.** Enter your account number and IFSC code for payouts.
5. **Set up shipping.** Choose how your orders will be picked up and delivered.
6. **Upload your signature** and accept the seller agreement.
7. **List your first product.** Your store goes live only after at least one product is listed.

## Common mistakes that delay new seller accounts

From our hands-on work with new sellers, these are the mistakes we see most often:

- **Name mismatch.** The name on the bank account, PAN and GST certificate do not match. Fix this before you start.
- **Inactive or wrong GSTIN.** A cancelled or suspended GST number will be rejected.
- **Blurry signature.** Amazon cannot use a dark, cropped or unclear signature image.
- **Personal email or number you later lose.** Changing these afterwards takes extra verification, so choose carefully.
- **Leaving the product step for later.** Many sellers finish registration but never list a product, so the store never goes live.

Each of these can turn a one-hour task into a week of back and forth with seller support.

A simple habit helps: before you open Seller Central, put your GST certificate, PAN card, a cancelled cheque or bank statement and your signature image in one folder on your phone or computer. Check that the names and addresses match across all of them. This five-minute check saves more time than anything else on this list.
