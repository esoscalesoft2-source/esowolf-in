# ESOWOLF blog — writing rules (for people)

These are the same rules the SEO score in /admin/ checks. Aim for **80% or more** before publishing.

## Who we write for
Indian online sellers and small brands on Amazon, Meesho, Myntra and Shopify. Many read English as a second language — write plainly.

## The rules
1. **Title**: 30–65 characters, main keyword near the start.
2. **Meta description**: 120–160 characters, includes the main keyword.
3. **Answer first**: use the main keyword in the first 2–3 sentences and answer the question straight away.
4. **Sections**: 4–8 `##` headings. No `#` title line (the title field is the heading).
5. **Length**: 1,000–1,500 words (1,600–2,200 for a big guide).
6. **One table** — a comparison, checklist or steps.
7. **Internal links**: 4–8, only from the allowed list (see the prompt / `admin/blog-config.json`), always including the topic group's main guide.
8. **FAQs**: 4–6 short questions and answers.
9. **Cover image** with alt text that describes the picture.
10. **Banned words**: delve, game-changer, revolutionize, unlock, "in today's fast-paced world".
11. **No ESOWOLF prices.** Platform fees in ₹ are fine when you are sure, but tell readers to check the latest seller help page.
12. **Only true facts** about ESOWOLF (listed in the prompt). No invented numbers, reviews, awards or customer counts. Never promise guaranteed sales or rankings, and never say we are an official partner of Amazon, Meesho, Myntra, Shopify or Meta.
13. Short paragraphs (2–3 sentences), friendly tone, Indian examples where useful.
14. End with a short honest "How ESOWOLF helps" section and one clear next step (webinar or meeting).

## Extras
- A YouTube link on its own line becomes a click-to-play video.
- `![description](https://…/video.mp4)` becomes an inline video.
- Short link code (e.g. `amazon-start`) gives you `esowolf.in/go/amazon-start` for Instagram bio and stories, with UTM tags added.
