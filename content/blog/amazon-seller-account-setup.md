## Why new sellers find Amazon seller account setup hard

Registering on Amazon looks easy at first. You go to sell.amazon.in, click "Start Selling" and fill in a few forms. But most first-time sellers face the same problems:

- They are not sure which documents Amazon will ask for.
- A small mistake in GST or bank details can delay the account.
- They don't know which shipping option to choose.
- Amazon will not launch the store until at least one product is listed, and the product form has many fields that are new to them.

Because of this, many people start the process and then leave it half-done. Our onboarding service is made for exactly this stage. You don't have to figure everything out alone.

## What you need to keep ready

Before we start, keep these details ready. Having them in one place makes the setup much faster.

| What you need | Why Amazon asks for it |
|---|---|
| Mobile number and email ID | To create your seller login and send OTPs |
| GSTIN and GST certificate | To verify your business (needed for most categories) |
| Business bank account details | Amazon pays your sales money into this account |
| Pickup address | Where orders are picked up; it must be in the same state as your GST registration |
| Store name | The name buyers will see on Amazon |
| First product details and photos | To list your first product so the store can go live |

If you only sell GST-exempt products such as books, Amazon lets you register without a GST number. For most other products, you will need your GSTIN.

## Step-by-step: how we set up your Amazon seller account

Here is the complete process we follow with you. Amazon's screens can change from time to time, but the main steps stay the same.

**Step 1: Start the registration.** We start on sell.amazon.in with your mobile number and email ID. If you already shop on Amazon.in, you can use the same login and verify it with an OTP.

**Step 2: Add your GST details.** We enter your GSTIN exactly as it appears on your certificate and upload the certificate. Amazon can take up to 72 hours to verify GST, so we continue with the other steps in the meantime.

**Step 3: Choose your store name.** We help you pick a clear, easy-to-remember store name. You can change it later in your settings if needed.

**Step 4: Add your pickup address.** We add the address from your GST details or a new one. Remember, the pickup address must be in the same state as your GST registration.

**Step 5: Pick a shipping method.** Amazon offers Easy Ship, where Amazon picks up and delivers your orders, and Self Ship, where you deliver the orders yourself or through a courier partner. We explain both in simple words so you can choose what suits your business.

**Step 6: Add your bank details.** We add your business bank account carefully and double-check it, because this is where your sales money will come.

**Step 7: Enter your default GST rate.** We set the GST rate that applies to your products, so your listings are created correctly.

Throughout the setup, the account stays in your name and uses your own details. Once it is done, we suggest you change your password and keep your login safe.

## Common mistakes our onboarding helps you avoid

Even small mistakes can slow down a new Amazon store. Our onboarding helps you avoid these:

- **Wrong GST details.** The GSTIN must match your certificate exactly, or verification can fail.
- **Pickup address in another state.** It must be in the same state as your GST registration.
- **Wrong bank details.** One wrong digit can stop your payments.
- **Weak first listing.** A short title, blurry photos or the wrong category make your product hard to find.
- **Leaving the setup half-done.** Your store does not go live until a product is listed, so we finish that step with you.
